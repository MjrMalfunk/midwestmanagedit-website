Review areas

We check the practical things that cause real downtime.

This is not a scary audit or a sales trap. It is a focused review of where technology supports the business, where risk may be hiding, and what should be handled first.

Devices & support pain points

Which computers, servers, printers, and network devices matter most, what keeps breaking, and who needs help most often.

Microsoft 365 & cloud accounts

Email, users, MFA, admin access, shared files, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams, and the account settings that protect daily work.

Backup & recovery readiness

Whether important files, devices, cloud data, and servers can be recovered if something fails, is deleted, or gets encrypted.

Security gaps

Endpoint protection, DNS filtering, risky sign-ins, old devices, missing updates, weak account controls, and avoidable exposure.

Maintenance & monitoring

How updates, alerts, device health, and support history can be tracked instead of handled only after something breaks.

Next-step priorities

Why it matters

Most IT problems are easier to fix before they become emergencies.

A failed backup, compromised account, slow computer, offline server, or missing admin password can stop work fast. A short review helps uncover weak spots while there is still time to plan.

  • Find backup gaps before a restore is needed.
  • Spot account and device risks before they turn into incidents.
  • Document the systems your business depends on.
  • Create a practical support path for your team.

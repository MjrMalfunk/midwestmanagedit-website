Frequently asked
Is cybersecurity only about antivirus?

No. Endpoint protection matters, but so do safer defaults, hardening, account hygiene, and the way support work is handled every day.

Do small businesses really need layered protection?

Yes. Small environments still carry real risk. The difference is that the solution should be right-sized and practical, not oversized and theatrical.

Can security be added without overcomplicating everything?

That is the goal. Better protection should make the environment steadier, not bury the business under unnecessary complexity.

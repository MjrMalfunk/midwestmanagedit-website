Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Midwest Managed IT is a business of LnK Consulting LLC.

Midwest Managed IT provides managed IT services, cybersecurity support, Microsoft 365 administration, backup guidance, and responsive support for small businesses. This site is used to describe the business, publish resources, answer buyer questions, collect inquiries, and support scheduling.

2. Information you provide

We collect the information you choose to send through the site.

This can include your name, company name, email address, phone number, approximate team size, service interests, business profile, current support setup, freeform notes, and any scheduling or estimate details you submit through the site.

When you use the live scheduler, the site may send booking information to Microsoft 365 / Microsoft Graph so an event can be created on the scheduling calendar and, when enabled, a Microsoft Teams meeting can be generated.

When you use the launch-updates signup, your contact details may be sent to the email-delivery or subscription platform used by the site to manage updates and confirmations.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most sites, this one also produces technical breadcrumbs.

  • IP address, browser details, device information, and basic request metadata
  • Pages visited, referring paths, and general site-usage signals
  • Hosting and server logs used to secure, troubleshoot, and operate the site

If analytics, advertising, or other tracking tools are added later, this policy may be updated to reflect those tools.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond, schedule, improve, and protect.

  • Responding to inquiries and scheduling conversations
  • Creating or managing calendar bookings and meeting links
  • Reviewing business fit, support needs, and likely next steps
  • Sending updates or confirmations you specifically requested
  • Improving the site, tools, and buyer experience
  • Maintaining security, preventing abuse, and protecting our systems
  • Meeting legal obligations and protecting our rights

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How information may be shared

Information may flow through the business tools that keep the site working.

We may share or process information with service providers and platforms that support hosting, scheduling, calendar booking, communications, or security. This can include Microsoft 365, Microsoft Graph, Microsoft Teams, hosting providers, email-delivery or subscription providers, and similar vendors that help operate the site or communications around it.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, systems, property, or safety.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as it is reasonably useful or required.

Retention periods can vary depending on the nature of the inquiry, booking activity, legal requirements, security needs, and whether a relationship continues beyond the website conversation.

7. Your choices

You control what you submit.

You can choose not to provide information through the site, but that may limit our ability to respond, schedule, or provide a useful follow-up. If you want us to stop using a prior inquiry for follow-up, contact us and we will make reasonable efforts to honor the request where appropriate.

8. Security

We take reasonable security steps, but no internet system is perfect.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to help protect information handled through the site. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

9. Children’s privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the site, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes and contact

Policies can change as the site evolves.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the site, the business, legal requirements, or the third-party services used to operate the experience. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@midwestmanagedit.com.

Service plans

Three support lanes, built to fit the real world.

Midwest Managed IT uses three plan lanes to keep the relationship understandable: a steady operating baseline, a stronger security-forward build, and a broader administrative and governance path for growing businesses.

Quick take

How to think about the plans

Manage IT creates a cleaner baseline. Protect IT is the strongest fit for many small businesses because security and backup coverage stop being optional side quests. Govern IT extends administration, coordination, and oversight for environments that need more lift.

Plan comparison

Pick the lane that matches the weight of the environment.

This is a relationship framework, not a mystery box. The goal is to help buyers understand what gets deeper as the service matures, without forcing the website into a thousand-line pricing chart.

Manage IT

Operational baseline

For organizations that want a steadier environment, proactive support, and a cleaner day-to-day operating rhythm.

  • Monitoring, maintenance, and support structure
  • Endpoint management and patching discipline
  • Remote-first help desk flow
  • Good fit for smaller teams building a stronger baseline
Talk through fit →
Protect IT

Security-forward support

The strongest overall lead. Built for small businesses that want support, protection, and backup coverage moving together instead of being stitched together later.

  • Everything in Manage IT, with stronger protection layers
  • Backup included as part of the support posture
  • Better fit for risk-aware teams and growing businesses
  • Usually the cleanest balance of value and readiness
Talk through fit →
Govern IT

Broader oversight

For environments that need more administrative depth, more coordination, and a stronger governance story around users, systems, and business process.

  • Everything in Protect IT, plus broader admin lift
  • Stronger fit for businesses with more moving parts
  • More room for governance-minded standards
  • Built for teams that need depth, not just response

How to choose

Choose by friction level, not by vanity.

Manage ITBest when the priority is steadier support, patching, maintenance, and a more organized baseline.
Protect ITBest when support alone is not enough and the business wants protection and backup to live inside the service relationship.
Govern ITBest when cloud admin, user lifecycle, policy, and broader coordination need more attention.
Not sure?That is normal. The first conversation can sort the lane without forcing a premature decision.
Who usually fits where
Manage ITSmaller environments that want less drag, fewer recurring problems, and stronger operational consistency.
Protect ITGrowing businesses that want security, backup, and support moving in the same direction instead of being patched together over time.
Govern ITTeams with more cloud administration, more user complexity, or more need for process and oversight.

Midwest Managed IT

Midwest Managed IT provides proactive IT support, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 administration, backup solutions, and responsive remote support for small businesses. We help organizations reduce downtime, improve security, and stay organized with managed technology services built around prevention, stability, and practical support. From day-to-day IT management and endpoint protection to cloud administration, backup oversight, and user support, we focus on keeping business systems running smoothly without the chaos of reactive break-fix service. Local on-site support may be available when needed, with remote-first service designed for speed, consistency, and long-term reliability.

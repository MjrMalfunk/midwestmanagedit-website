What backup and recovery means

A practical safety net for business data and continuity.

Backup and recovery is the process of protecting important business data and making sure there is a usable path to restore it when something goes wrong. Recovery readiness goes a step further by asking whether the right data is covered, whether backup health is visible, and whether the business understands what recovery may look like before there is an emergency.

How backup and recovery readiness works

Protection, visibility, and planning working together.

  • Identify the systems, files, and cloud data that matter most
  • Keep backup coverage and health easier to see
  • Watch for missed backups, failures, or unusual backup concerns
  • Connect backup planning with cybersecurity and support
  • Talk through restore expectations before pressure hits
  • Keep recovery planning practical for the size of the business

Frequently Asked
Is backup the same thing as recovery readiness?

Not quite. Backup protects data, but recovery readiness also looks at coverage, backup health, restore expectations, and whether the business has a practical path to recover when something goes wrong.

Do small businesses really need backup and recovery planning?

Yes. Small businesses still depend on files, email, cloud tools, devices, accounting data, customer records, and daily operations. A basic recovery plan helps reduce confusion when something important becomes unavailable.

Can backup and cybersecurity be planned together?

They should be. Cybersecurity helps reduce risk, while backup and recovery readiness help the business recover if files are deleted, systems fail, or an incident affects normal work.

What if we already have a backup tool?

That is a good starting point. The next step is understanding what is protected, whether backups are healthy, what is not covered, and how recovery would actually be handled.

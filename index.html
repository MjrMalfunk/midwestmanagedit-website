FIRST, THE PLAIN-ENGLISH VERSION

What is an MSP?
Your ongoing IT partner.

MSP stands for managed service provider. It’s a team you work with to keep your business technology maintained, supported, and ready for everyday work.

That can include looking after computers, helping your people, managing Microsoft 365, watching over security, and checking that recovery plans make sense.

At MMIT, we help you understand what’s covered, where the gaps are, and what to tackle next. The exact scope depends on the services and plan you choose.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

More answers ↗
Do I need a full managed IT plan?

Tell us what you need first. We can help determine whether ongoing managed services or a more specific scope is the right conversation.

Can you help if we already have IT support?

Yes. Tell us what is already covered and where you need help. We can discuss the gaps and responsibilities before proposing a scope.

Do you only work onsite?

We use a remote-first support model, with local onsite support when the work calls for it. Location and scope are part of the initial discussion.

Will I need to book a call right away?

No. Send your information and we’ll follow up by email. We can arrange a conversation when it makes sense.

What if I’m already a client?

Use the client portal for support requests so they reach the existing support workflow.

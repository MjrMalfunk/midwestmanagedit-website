Frequently asked
Is backup the same thing as recovery readiness?

Not quite. Having a backup tool is one piece. Knowing what is covered, how healthy it is, and what recovery looks like is the bigger picture.

Can backup planning scale for smaller businesses?

Yes. The goal is practical continuity, not pretending every company needs an enterprise war room.

Can backup and security be planned together?

They should be. Protection and recovery are stronger when they are treated as parts of the same resilience story.
